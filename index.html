About

Projects

Coffeeshop Concerts

In the Spring of 2023, I started a foundation called Coffeeshop Concerts. We run a concert series hosted in local cafes with goals of:

  • Providing performance experience for aspiring musicians
  • Bringing live music into my community
  • Giving back to youth music programs through fundraising

To date, we have hosted over 40 concerts and raised nearly $4,000.

Spartan Music Business Club

This fall, we have officially launched the Spartan Music Business Club.

We target professional development through a guest speaker series with industry professionals and hands-on experience through concert production and live music booking.
